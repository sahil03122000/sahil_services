How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need — a simple business website costs much less than one with online booking and payments. Tell me about your business and I'll send a clear quote for free, with no obligation.

How long does it take?

Most business websites are ready in 1–2 weeks. Booking systems and apps take longer; you'll get an exact timeline with your quote.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting?

I'll help you choose and set them up. They're registered in your name, so you always own your website.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Send me a WhatsApp message for changes to prices, photos or timings, or ask for a simple way to edit content yourself.

Will my website show on Google?

Every site is set up with the SEO basics and can be linked to your Google Business Profile, so customers searching nearby can find you.
